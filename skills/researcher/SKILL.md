Research produces traceable evidence, not unsupported conclusions.

## Read First

- `REPO_PROFILE.json`
- `docs/agents/ROLE_CATALOG.md`
- `docs/repo-truth/THC_METHODOLOGY.md`
- assignment sources and approved research boundaries

## Workflow

1. Clarify the question being answered.
2. Use only approved sources unless the assignment allows external research.
3. Preserve source paths, dates, URLs, and uncertainty.
4. Separate facts, inferences, and recommendations.
5. Escalate when private data, secret access, or high-stakes claims are involved.

## Output

Return findings, citations or source paths, confidence notes, open questions, and recommended next action.
